The Locked Room

The rain had been falling for three hours when you arrived at Blackwood Manor.

At exactly midnight, the old grandfather clock stopped.

Then came the scream.

You follow the sound upstairs and discover Mr. Blackwood, the owner of the mansion, lying motionless inside his study.

The strange part?

The study is locked from the inside.

There are no open windows. No secret passage. No weapon.

Three people were in the mansion tonight.

  • Mr. Blackwood's daughter, Clara
  • The butler, Thomas
  • The gardener, Edward

Someone is lying.

Examine the grandfather clock.
Investigate the study.
Question Clara.

The Grandfather Clock

You examine the clock carefully.

The hands are frozen at exactly 12:00. But something catches your attention.

A tiny smear of red paint covers the minute hand.

Clue discovered: Red paint on the clock.

You remember seeing a freshly painted red door somewhere in the mansion.

Return to the study.
Investigate the garden.

The Locked Study

You inspect the room.

The body lies beside the desk. On the desk is an empty glass, an open book, and a small brass key.

The window is locked.

You notice something unusual beneath the desk.

A thin piece of black thread is caught around one of the legs.

Clue discovered: Black thread.

Examine the brass key.
Question Clara about the room.
Question Thomas, the butler.

The Brass Key

The key doesn't belong to the study.

You recognize its shape.

It belongs to the small storage room beside the kitchen.

Why would Mr. Blackwood have the key in his study?

Go to the storage room.
Ask Thomas about the key.

Clara

Clara sits quietly in the hallway.

"I heard the scream," she says. "But I never went near the study."

You notice that her dress has a small tear near the sleeve.

There is a tiny piece of black thread hanging from it.

Clue discovered: Clara's dress contains black thread.

Clara notices you staring at the tear.

"I caught it on the garden gate," she says quickly.

Check the garden gate.
Accuse Clara.

Thomas the Butler

Thomas looks nervous when you mention the key.

"Mr. Blackwood always kept that key in the study," he says.

You know this is a lie.

The key clearly belongs to the storage room.

"Perhaps you should ask the gardener," Thomas says.

He quickly walks away.

Follow Thomas toward the garden.
Investigate the storage room.

The Garden Gate

Outside, the rain has turned the garden into mud.

You examine the red-painted garden gate.

There is black thread caught on one of its hinges.

Clue discovered: Black thread on the garden gate.

Clara was telling the truth about catching her dress on the gate.

But that doesn't explain how the thread got inside the locked study.

Search the storage room.
Accuse Clara.

The Storage Room

You unlock the storage room.

Inside, you find gardening tools, old furniture, and a coil of black thread.

But there is something else.

A small section of the wall has been recently disturbed.

Behind it is a narrow passage leading directly toward the study.

Clue discovered: A hidden passage.

The locked-room mystery wasn't a locked-room mystery at all.

Follow the passage.

Wrong Accusation

You accuse Clara.

She looks shocked.

Moments later, you discover the hidden passage behind the storage room.

Clara could not have used it.

You accused the wrong person.

THE END

The Wrong Suspect

You accuse Clara based on the thread.

She denies everything.

Then you notice something.

The black thread on the garden gate matches Clara's dress, but not the thread found inside the study.

Someone else planted the evidence.

You realize the real culprit is still inside the mansion.

Question Thomas again.
Search the storage room.

The Truth

You enter the hidden passage.

At the other end, you find Thomas.

He is holding a length of black thread.

"You weren't supposed to find this," he whispers.

Thomas had entered the study through the hidden passage, poisoned Mr. Blackwood, and escaped before anyone arrived.

The locked door was only an illusion.

The clock had been stopped to create a false timeline.

And the black thread was used to pull the study door shut from the passage.

You finally understand.

The Butler Did It.

You solved the mystery.

THE END
