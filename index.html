The Door

I was walking home when I saw a door in the middle of the sidewalk.

It wasn't attached to anything. Just a door.

I probably should have kept walking, but obviously I didn't.

I opened the door.

I decided to leave.

Behind the Door

There was a hallway behind it. It was really long and there were no lights.

I could hear something at the end.

I walked down the hallway.

I closed the door.

The Hallway

I walked for what felt like forever.

Then I saw a window.

Outside the window was my house.

The weird part was that I could see myself sitting inside my bedroom.

I looked closer.

My Room

The person sitting in my room looked up at the window.

It was me.

Then the other me smiled.

I decided I had seen enough.

I ran back.

The Door Closes

I slammed the door shut.

When I looked again, there was nothing there.

Just the sidewalk.

I went home and tried not to think about it.

End.

Walking Away

I decided that finding a random door in the sidewalk was probably not my problem.

So I walked home.

Everything was normal.

Mostly.

When I got to my bedroom, there was a door in the wall that wasn't there before.

I opened it.

The End

I don't really know what happened after that.

Maybe I should have never opened the door.

But I guess that's what happens when you get curious.
