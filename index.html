The Other Side

Maya slowly turned the handle. The door opened with a long, quiet creak.

On the other side was her childhood bedroom. Everything looked exactly as it had twenty years ago.

On the desk was a photograph of Maya as a child. Beside it was a note with her name written on it.

She could:

Read the note or Close the door.

The Path Home

Maya decided that some mysteries were better left alone. She turned around and followed the trail back through the woods.

But after a few minutes, she realized something strange. The path she had followed was no longer there.

Instead, she found another door.

Open it or Keep walking.

The Note

Maya unfolded the note.

"If you're reading this, you finally remembered."

Maya stared at the words. She suddenly remembered the woods, the door, and a promise she had made as a child.

At the bottom of the page was another choice:

Remember everything or Forget.

The Choice

Maya stepped backward and closed the door. The moment it shut, the forest became completely silent.

When she turned around, the door was gone.

Perhaps some doors only appear once.

The Memory

Maya remembered.

She remembered that she had built the door herself when she was eight years old.

She remembered making a promise to return. And now she understood why.

The door wasn't leading into the past.

It was leading somewhere else.

The End

Maya kept walking.

Eventually, the trees disappeared and she found herself standing at the edge of town.

She never saw the mysterious door again.

But sometimes, late at night, she dreamed about it.

And in her dreams, the door was always open.
